04 / WHAT'S REAL

The evidence has boundaries.

01Operator-controlled records

The report contains controller-observed checks and linked digests, not third-party attestation. The viewer does not authenticate the host or Meta provenance.

02No public execution

This site cannot access Docker, run a model, alter infrastructure or upload your local report. Verification and fault experiments remain local and opt-in.

03Limited experimental scope

DNS transfer has been physically tested with two predetermined candidates. A global immune network and autonomous prevention remain future research.
